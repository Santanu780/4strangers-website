01 — THE STORY

Four strangers.
One place.

We started with a simple curiosity: what happens when the ingredients around us are given a different voice?

From Assam's rice and tea to its fruits, citrus and wild botanicals, 4Strangers explores fermentation as a meeting point between heritage and experimentation.
